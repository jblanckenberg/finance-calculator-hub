Advertising & Affiliate Disclosure

FinCalcHub is free to use. We pay for it by running Google AdSense. Here's the full picture — what we earn from, what we don't, and how that affects what you read.

1. Display advertising (Google AdSense)

FinCalcHub runs display ads supplied by Google AdSense (client ca-pub-5092336325075679). Ads appear in fixed slots — typically a banner above and below the main content. We don't allow interstitial, pop-up, or video-takeover ads.

Google selects which ads to show. The selection is automated and influenced by your browsing history; we have no say in it. We're not paid more for showing one advertiser's ad over another. You can opt out of personalised ads at google.com/settings/ads.

2. Affiliate links

At present, FinCalcHub does not use affiliate links. If we ever do — for example, recommending a high-interest savings account or a low-cost investment platform — every affiliate link will:

  • Be marked clearly within the article ("affiliate link" or equivalent).
  • Use the rel="sponsored" attribute as required by Google's webmaster guidelines.
  • Only point to products we have personally used or independently verified.
  • Be listed on this page, with the commercial relationship disclosed.

3. Sponsored content

We don't currently publish sponsored articles, paid reviews, or "advertorial" content. If we did, it would be labelled "Sponsored" in the page title and at the top of the body, and listed here.

4. Editorial independence

Our advertising and any future affiliate relationships have no influence on what we cover, how we explain a topic, or how we rank options. Calculators run the actual formula whether or not an advertiser is currently bidding on a related keyword.

The fastest way to see how we work is the editorial policy — it details our sourcing, review, and correction process.

5. Compliance

This disclosure is published in line with:

  • The U.S. Federal Trade Commission's Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising.
  • The UK Advertising Standards Authority (ASA) and the Competition and Markets Authority (CMA) influencer-marketing guidance.
  • The South African Advertising Regulatory Board's disclosure principles for online content.
