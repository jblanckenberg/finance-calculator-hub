# Content Gap Analysis — finncalc.com

> **Source:** Firecrawl SERP scans, 2026-05-12.
> **Method:** Top 5 organic results across 8 priority calculator topics (UK + US + SA Google).
> **Owner:** James Blanckenberg.

## Executive summary

**Competitive landscape (8 SERPs, 40 ranking URLs):**

| Site | Times in top 5 | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **calculator.net** | 4 | Generic calculator hub, biggest direct competitor |
| **NerdWallet** | 4 | US-dominant, strong brand, DR ~90 |
| **Bankrate** | 3 | US, DR ~90, owned by Red Ventures |
| **Ramsey Solutions** | 2 | US, debt-snowball focus, DR ~80 |
| **ADP** | 2 | Payroll, take-home pay |
| **Investor.gov, MoneyHelper, SARS** | 1 each | Gov sources — authoritative but rigid |
| **UK / SA banks** (Nationwide, Barclays, Old Mutual) | 4 combined | Lender-branded calculators dominate regional finance SERPs |
| **Brokerages** (Schwab, Vanguard, Ameriprise) | 4 combined | Tool roundups, less direct competition |
| **MoneySavingExpert** | 1 | UK authority — high DR, hard to displace |

**Headline finding:** finncalc.com competes against 3 categories of incumbent: (a) huge generic-calculator hubs (calculator.net), (b) high-DR personal-finance publishers (NerdWallet, Bankrate), and (c) lender/brokerage branded tools. Beating any of these head-on for "compound interest calculator" or "mortgage calculator" is a 12-24 month effort. **The winnable space is regional sub-queries, cross-region comparison content, and the long tail.**

## Strategic positioning

### Finncalc's structural advantages
1. **Three regions in one calculator.** No competitor offers USA + UK + SA in a single tool. NerdWallet is US-only; MoneySavingExpert is UK-only; Old Mutual is SA-only.
2. **Zero signup wall.** Every brokerage tool (Schwab, Vanguard, Ameriprise) gates results. We don't.
3. **No upsell.** Lenders show calculators to capture leads. We don't have a sales motion to protect.
4. **Cross-border audience.** Our visitor will plausibly want to compare regions (emigration, retirement abroad, contract work) — a use case the incumbents structurally cannot serve.

### Finncalc's structural disadvantages
1. **Brand-new domain.** DR ~0 vs DR 75-95 for incumbents. Will take 12-18 months of consistent backlinks to compete head-on for high-volume queries.
2. **No editorial team.** NerdWallet has 200+ writers; SmartAsset has a research arm. We're solo.
3. **No paid marketing.** Incumbents bid on these keywords plus own organic.

### Strategic verdict
Don't fight head-on for "compound interest calculator". Instead:
- Own the **long tail of regional + multi-region queries** ("compound interest UK vs USA", "salary R500,000 take-home South Africa", "401k contribution paycheck calculator").
- Own the **cross-region comparison niche** that nobody else can serve.
- Use **South Africa as a beachhead** — it's underserved and high-intent.
- Build calculator-specific glossary content to capture definitional queries (which AI overviews love).

## What this analysis doesn't tell us

- **Search volume per query** — needs Ahrefs / SEMrush / Keyword Planner (free tier returns ranges only).
- **Keyword difficulty (KD)** — same. SERP composition is a proxy but not a number.
- **Click-through rates by position** — needs GSC data after a few weeks of indexing.
- **Backlink targets** — these top-ranking pages all have referring-domain counts we'd want to surface for outreach. Pulling that takes paid SEO tools or 100+ free-tier checks.

These are the natural next steps when budget allows an SEO subscription. Until then, the regional-niche strategy above doesn't depend on any of them.
