Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2024

1. Information We Collect

FinCalcHub does not collect any personal information. All calculations are performed entirely within your browser. No financial data you enter into our calculators is transmitted to our servers.

2. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use our site. This collects anonymous data such as pages visited, time spent on pages, and general location (country/city level). No personally identifiable information is collected. You can opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

3. Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google uses cookies to show relevant ads based on your browsing history. You can opt out of personalised advertising at google.com/settings/ads.

4. Cookies

We use a single first-party cookie to remember your preferred region (USA, UK, or South Africa). This cookie contains no personal information. Google Analytics and AdSense set their own cookies as described in Google's privacy policy.
